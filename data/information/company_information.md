# Power Gym Budibazar Pokhara

## 1. Organization Overview

Power Gym Budibazar Pokhara is a fitness center located in
Budibazar, Pokhara-26, Kaski, Nepal. The gym provides a fitness
environment for people who want to improve their physical fitness,
strength, health, and overall well-being.

The gym is open every day from 5:00 AM to 9:00 PM, allowing members
to exercise before or after their work and study hours.

Power Gym Budibazar Pokhara also maintains an online presence through
social media, particularly Facebook, where it shares information and
updates with its audience. Its Facebook page has approximately 1.7K
followers.

With the increasing use of smartphones, the organization identified
an opportunity to strengthen its digital presence through a dedicated
mobile application. The development of this mobile application was
the focus of the internship project.

## 3. Organization Hierarchy

The organizational structure of Power Gym Budibazar Pokhara consists
of the gym management and fitness-related staff responsible for the
daily operation of the gym.

The gym owner, who serves as the partnership head, is at the top of
the organizational structure. The manager works under the owner and
coordinates the different teams. The management oversees the overall
operation of the gym, while trainers and fitness staff assist members
with their fitness activities and workouts.

The following teams work under the manager:

### Front Desk Staff

The front desk staff welcome members and visitors, handle inquiries,
and manage membership-related activities at the reception.

### Trainer Team

The trainer team consists of fitness trainers, personal trainers,
and a Zumba instructor who guide members in their workouts and
fitness activities.

### Maintenance Team

The maintenance team is responsible for cleanliness and the
maintenance of gym equipment and facilities.

### Digitalization

The digitalization unit is responsible for developing and maintaining
the gym's digital presence. The mobile application development
internship was carried out under this unit.

## 4. Working Domains of the Organization

Power Gym Budibazar Pokhara operates in the fitness and wellness
sector. Its major working domains include:

- Gym and strength training facilities
- Fitness training
- Personal training
- Group fitness activities such as Zumba
- Membership management
- Front-desk services
- Gym equipment and facility maintenance
- Social media communication and promotion
- Digitalization through the mobile application project
