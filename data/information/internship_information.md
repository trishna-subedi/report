## Intern Department / Unit

During the internship, I worked as a Mobile Application Developer
Intern in the digitalization unit of Power Gym Budibazar Pokhara.
The unit works directly with the manager and gym management.

The purpose of this unit is to move the gym's information and
communication to digital platforms so that members can access
information more easily.

I worked under the guidance of my mentor, Mr. Krishna Subedi, and
coordinated with the gym management, trainers, and front-desk staff
to collect the information required for the mobile application.
